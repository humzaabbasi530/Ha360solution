Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how our Google Play Store applications handle your data.

Effective Date: July 24, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Ha360solutions (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates mobile applications published on the Google Play Store, including WhatsApp Status Saver, PDF Reader, and Phone Cleaner (collectively, “our Apps”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Apps.

By downloading and using any of our Apps you agree to the terms outlined below. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

Our Apps are built with a privacy-first approach. We minimise data collection to only what is necessary:

Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and unique identifiers for crash reporting & analytics.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics (features used, session duration, error logs) to improve performance.
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID may be collected to serve personalised or non-personalised ads.

NOT Collected

  • We do NOT collect personal data such as your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • We do NOT access, store, or transmit your WhatsApp messages, contacts, or private media to our servers.
  • We do NOT collect the content of PDF documents you view.
  • We do NOT collect personal files during cleaning operations.
  • App Functionality: Ensuring our Apps work correctly on your device.
  • Performance Improvement: Analysing crash reports, identifying bugs, and optimising performance.
  • Advertising: Displaying ads via third-party networks (e.g., Google AdMob) to support free app development.
  • Compliance: Meeting legal obligations and protecting our rights.

Our Apps may use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services – Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – Privacy Info
  • Firebase Analytics – Privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics – Privacy

WhatsApp Status Saver

  • Storage: Access & save WhatsApp status media locally. No media is uploaded to any server.

PDF Reader

  • Storage: Open, read & manage PDF files on your device. Contents never leave the device.

Phone Cleaner

  • Storage: Scan & remove junk files, cache & temporary data.
  • Usage Access (optional): For accurate app-level storage analysis — used locally only.
  • All operations are performed locally on your device.
  • We do not operate external servers that store your personal data.
  • Third-party SDKs follow industry-standard security practices.
  • We regularly update our Apps to patch vulnerabilities.

No method of electronic storage is 100 % secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

  • Local Data: Delete by clearing app data or uninstalling.
  • Analytics: Retained by Firebase per their retention policies.
  • Ad Data: Reset your Advertising ID or opt out via device settings.
  • Access: Request what data, if any, we hold.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of any associated data.
  • Opt-out: Adjust personalised ads in device settings.
  • Revoke Permissions: Via Settings → Apps at any time.
